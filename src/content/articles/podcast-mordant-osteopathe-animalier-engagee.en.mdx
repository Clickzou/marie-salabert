Following SymbiOsteo 2 – The International Congress of Animal Osteopathy, a second episode of the Mordant podcast, hosted by animal osteopath Mathilde Chalut Natal, has been released on the various listening platforms.

In this conversation, we take a more personal look back at my journey in animal osteopathy, from my years of study to my current professional practice. This episode covers, among other things, my involvement within UFEOA, my sitting of the national aptitude examination, as well as the various experiences that have shaped my professional development over the years.

We also discuss my involvement within the collective of animal osteopaths and the thinking that led me to take part in structuring and representing the profession.

During this episode, I also share the reasons that led me to help organise the various SymbiOsteo congresses, as well as the motivations that have driven me for several years now in my practice and my commitment to animal osteopathy.

This episode is available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and the main listening platforms.
