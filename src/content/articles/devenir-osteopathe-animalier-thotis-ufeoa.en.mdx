In 2020, Thotis and the UFEOA carried out an interview dedicated to the animal osteopathy sector, in order to provide clear and accessible information to future students.

Aimed mainly at sixth-form students and those in the process of choosing their further education path, this video sets out to present how the sector works, the available training courses, and the regulatory and legislative framework governing animal osteopathy in France.

Through this discussion, the UFEOA sheds light on the challenges facing the profession, the organisation of schools, training requirements and changes in regulation, in order to help future candidates better understand this course of study and its professional prospects.
