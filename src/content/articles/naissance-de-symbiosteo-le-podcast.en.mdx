My involvement in the field of animal osteopathy has developed gradually over the years, alongside my professional career.

From 2017 to 2020, I was first involved with the UFEOA student association, a formative experience that allowed me to take part in structuring and representing students in the field.

From 2020 to 2025, this involvement continued within the collective of animal osteopaths, in a dynamic of reflection and action around the evolution of the profession and its challenges.

Alongside this associative involvement, I also helped organise two SymbiOsteo conferences, in 2019 and then in 2022, bringing together those working in animal osteopathy and animal health for discussions, talks and reflection on the discipline.

In early 2024, this project evolved into a new format with the creation of SymbiOsteo – the podcast. The aim of this media outlet is to give a voice to the various people working in animal osteopathy and animal health.

The podcast highlights varied and often unique professional journeys, with the aim of understanding each guest's path, from their starting point to their current practice, as well as the personal and professional motivations that guided their choices.

SymbiOsteo was born from a desire to share and pass on knowledge, as well as a deep wish to shine a light on the shared values that connect the various people working in animal health. It is also rooted in a strong passion for the profession and its development.

The podcast format includes several types of episodes: duo interviews, as well as round-table discussions bringing together several guests around shared themes.

The podcast is available on most listening platforms, including Spotify, Apple Podcasts and Deezer.
