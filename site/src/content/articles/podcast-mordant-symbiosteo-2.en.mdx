Ahead of SymbiOsteo 2 – The International Congress of Animal Osteopathy, I had the pleasure of being invited onto the Mordant podcast, hosted by animal osteopath Mathilde Chalut Natal.

Recorded a month before the Congress, this interview aimed to present and promote the event, looking back at its organisation, its ambitions and the themes that would be covered during the conferences.

This conversation offered the opportunity to present the objectives of SymbiOsteo 2, the schedule of the various days, as well as the diversity of speakers invited for this second edition. We also discussed the wish to bring together the different players in osteopathy and animal health around moments of discussion, reflection and shared experience.

This podcast thus offers a behind-the-scenes glimpse of the event and the spirit in which this international congress was conceived: fostering interprofessional dialogue, showcasing research initiatives and contributing to a better understanding of the current issues in animal osteopathy.

The episode is available to listen to on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and the main audio streaming platforms.
